Building the infrastructure for dharma in the modern world.

One platform where centers manage retreats, registrations, lodging, and meals — and practitioners discover teachings, find teachers, and deepen their practice.

We believe the operational work of running a dharma center is itself an act of practice. It deserves tools built with that understanding.

For Centers

Run your center, not your software.

A purpose-built operations platform. Events, registration, lodging, meals, payments, check-in — designed for how dharma centers actually work. Not a generic booking tool. Not a spreadsheet. Not five disconnected systems held together by email.

For Practitioners

Find your next retreat, teaching, or sitting.

A single place to discover retreats, teachings, and practice opportunities across your lineage and beyond. Register, manage your bookings, and stay connected to the communities that support your path.

Why “Joyful Effort”?

Vīrya — one of the six perfections in Mahayana Buddhism — is the energy of delighting in what is good. Not the grind of obligation, but the enthusiasm that comes from doing meaningful work.

We chose this name because it describes both what we’re building and how we want to build it.

Where we are

Joyful Effort is in active development, working with our first pilot centers in New York, London, France, and India. The platform is real, substantial, and being built in close partnership with the people who will use it daily.

If you run a dharma center and are curious about what we’re building, or if you’d like to follow our progress, we’d love to hear from you.
